Alegreya
--------

The three webfonts in this folder — Alegreya-Regular.woff2, Alegreya-Medium.woff2
and Alegreya-Bold.woff2 — are the Alegreya typeface by Juan Pablo del Peral and
the Alegreya Project Authors (Huerta Tipográfica).

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full licence text is
in OFL.txt beside this file, as clause 2 of that licence requires: the copyright
notice and the licence must travel with every copy of the font, original or
modified.

Source: https://github.com/huertatipografica/Alegreya
        https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alegreya

The fonts are converted to WOFF2 for web delivery and changed in no other way.
The OFL counts a format change as a "Modified Version" (clause 1, "or by
changing formats"), so this is not an unmodified redistribution. Alegreya's
notice declares no Reserved Font Name, so clause 3 leaves the family name
usable; clause 2's requirement — that the notice travel with every copy — is met
by OFL.txt shipping beside these files.
