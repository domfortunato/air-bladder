Air Bladder — licensing
=======================

THIS DISTRIBUTION IS NOT UNDER A SINGLE LICENCE.

The MIT text at the bottom of this file covers the SOFTWARE ONLY — the code that
makes up the Foundry VTT system. Everything else that ships alongside it is
licensed separately by its own author, on terms MIT neither restates nor
overrides. In particular, MIT grants no rights whatsoever over the Cairn game
text, the character art, the icons, the fonts, or the Air Bladder logo.

Air Bladder is a fan implementation of Cairn 2e by Yochai Gal. It is not
published, endorsed or approved by him.

Full credits, with links and attribution requirements, are in README.md under
"Credits & licenses". That section is canonical and this file mirrors it; if the
two ever disagree, README.md is right and this file is the bug.


What ships under which licence
------------------------------

Code — MIT
    module/  templates/  css/  tools/  lang/  system.json  and the build
    scripts. The MIT notice below applies to these and only these. The system
    code descends from Cairn-FoundryVTT by Yochai Gal & Oskar Świda (MIT),
    itself descended from the Electric Bastionland system.
    lang/ holds the interface strings. The Danish, French, German, Polish and
    Brazilian Portuguese files are inherited from Cairn-FoundryVTT and travel
    under its MIT licence; they are not actively maintained here. The Castilian
    files are covered by the Spanish clause below instead.
    The four Warden macros in src/packs/macros/ (and their compiled copies in
    packs/) are code too: original JavaScript written for this system, carrying
    no Cairn text. They travel under this MIT notice, not the game-text clause
    below — a pack is a container, not a licence boundary.
    The System Docs journals travel here for the same reason: they are this
    project's own Warden guides, generated from docs/ by
    tools/import/system-docs.mjs, and they reproduce no Cairn text.

Game text — CC BY-SA 4.0, © Yochai Gal
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    The Cairn rules and text (1st and 2nd edition), and so the compendium content
    in packs/ and src/packs/ that reproduces them: backgrounds, items, monsters,
    tables and their descriptions, and the JOURNALS that carry the rules text
    itself — the Cairn 2e Rules compendium holds "Overview & Principles" and
    "Core Rules for Players", transcribed from the 2e text and shipping as
    readable pages rather than as a link to it, and the Vald compendium holds
    the Warden's Guide setting chapter of the same name, fetched from the
    Cairn SRD by tools/import/vald.mjs.
    Two packs are not this: the macros pack holds no game text, and the System
    Docs journals are this project's own writing, generated from docs/ by
    tools/import/system-docs.mjs. Both are covered by the Code clause above.
    ATTRIBUTION IS REQUIRED AND SHAREALIKE IS NOT WAIVED. A derivative of this
    text must carry the same licence. The MIT grant below does not reach it and
    cannot be used to relicense it, including by a fork of this repository.

"Backgrounds for Cairn" — CC BY-SA 4.0 (text), © Gordon McCormick
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    The seven class backgrounds (Fighter, Cleric, Magic-User, Thief, Dwarf,
    Elf, Halfling) in the Backgrounds (Custom) compendium, their one-off gear
    in the Background Items compendium, and the Shield spellbook — transcribed
    from "Backgrounds for Cairn" by Gordon McCormick, itself based on Cairn by
    Yochai Gal and BECMI D&D by Frank Mentzer. Text only: THE BOOKLET'S ART
    (Perplexing Ruins, Jeff Koch) IS NOT COVERED by its text licence and none
    of it ships here. ShareAlike is not waived.

GLOG Magic — CC BY-SA 4.0
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    The 100 spells in the GLOG Spellscrolls compendium, transcribed verbatim
    from the official Cairn GLOG Magic hack's spell list
    (https://cairnrpg.com/hacks/glog-spells/, with the rules at
    https://cairnrpg.com/hacks/glog-magic/), whose pages state the CC BY-SA
    4.0 licence. The Mishaps table in Tables (GLOG) and the two journals in
    Journals (GLOG) — "GLOG Magic — Player Rules" and "GLOG Magic — Spells" —
    are the same hack's text under the same licence. [dice] and [sum] are the
    hack's casting variables and are part of the text. ShareAlike is not
    waived.

Spanish translation — CC BY-SA 4.0, © Malecho (https://github.com/fsmalecho)
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    lang/es.json (interface) and lang/content/es.json (game content) — the
    Castilian (es-ES) translation. As a derivative of the CC BY-SA game text it
    inherits that licence, ShareAlike included.

Cairn compatibility marks — CC BY-SA 4.0, © Yochai Gal
    logo/  — used per the terms at https://cairnrpg.com/resources/logos
    The "For Use With Cairn" stamp the sheets display, and the older
    "Compatible with Cairn 2e" badge it replaced. Ship them unmodified;
    see logo/README.md.

Air Bladder logo and monster art — © Lydia Comer. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
    art/lydia-comer/  — https://linktr.ee/lydiadidmyink
    NOT a Creative Commons licence, and not covered by MIT. Licensed to the Air
    Bladder system for inclusion and redistribution as part of the system and
    its forks, and for use representing and promoting the project — the project
    website, package listings, and release announcements. Any use outside the
    Air Bladder project requires the artist's permission.
    Two jobs, one grant. The logo files sit at the top of the folder; below
    them, art/lydia-comer/portraits/ and art/lydia-comer/tokens/ hold 17
    creatures drawn for this system, offered in the portrait picker and paired
    portrait-to-token by filename stem. They ship re-encoded to WebP q95, the
    tokens/ half sized for the canvas (400×400); beyond that the artwork is
    left as drawn — this project's own practice, not a term of the grant,
    which bounds USE, not modification.
    Contents: art/lydia-comer/CREDITS.md.
    Full terms: art/lydia-comer/license.txt.

Character portraits and tokens — CC BY 4.0, © Jon Aspeheim
    art/jon-aspeheim/portraits/  art/jon-aspeheim/tokens/
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    https://jonaspeheim.itch.io/lemurs-portraits
    Redistribution is fine; attribution is required. Keep the credit if the art
    ships. The artist states these were created without AI.
    MODIFIED: the source ships 1000×1000 PNG portraits and no tokens. The
    portraits/ half is re-encoded to WebP at the same size; the tokens/ half is
    derived from those portraits — cropped and downscaled to 256×256 for the
    canvas. This is the indication CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) requires.
    See art/jon-aspeheim/license.txt.

game-icons.net art — CC BY 3.0
    icons/       — the class icons, by Lorc, Delapouite, Skoll & SeregaCthtuf
    art/game-icons/  — the Game-Icons picker gallery, 2,275 glyphs in 38
                   categories by 22 named contributors
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
    Two paths, one grant: same source, same licence, different jobs. Per-icon
    author and source-page attribution: icons/CREDITS.md and
    art/game-icons/CREDITS.md. Those files ARE the attribution — the shipped path
    records only the category, because the artist lives in the download's
    folder structure and the flatten discards it. The upstream notice ships
    verbatim as art/game-icons/license.txt.

Tlomdev's Tokens — CC BY-SA 4.0, © 2023 tlomdev
    art/tlomdev/
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    https://tlomdev.itch.io/tlomdevs-tokens
    368 token drawings in the Tlomdev picker gallery, kept in the artist's own
    category folders. MODIFIED: the 298 category drawings are re-encoded from
    PNG to WebP (quality 95) and changed in no other way — the indication
    CC BY-SA 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) requires, stated in full in the Modifications

    ShareAlike: adaptations of the art must carry this same licence.

Alegreya typeface — SIL Open Font License 1.1
    fonts/  — © 2011 The Alegreya Project Authors (Huerta Tipográfica)
    https://openfontlicense.org/
    Converted to WOFF2 for web delivery and changed in no other way. The OFL
