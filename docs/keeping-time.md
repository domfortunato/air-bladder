# Keeping Time

Cairn counts a journey in **watches**. The system tracks them, shows the current
watch to everyone at the table, and puts a Vald date on it if you're running
Vald.

---

## Watches

A day is three watches of eight hours: **morning**, **afternoon** and **night**.
Every character chooses one Wilderness Action per watch, and the last watch of
the day is usually spent making camp.

The travel tables count in watches. A trail costs **+1 Watch**, wilderness
**+2 Watches**, mountains another **+2**, and bad weather can add one more. Add
them up and you've got the length of the journey in watches. Divide by three
to get days.

Weather is rolled once a day.

## The calendar bar

A bar across the top of the screen shows everyone the current watch and the
date, and with the Vald calendar on, the season. Once you've rolled or set it,
today's weather shows under the bar. With the Vald calendar on, the date and
the season are buttons that open the month.

Only you can move the clock. Players can only read it.

**Turning it off.** Use **Configure Settings → Air Bladder → Configure General
Settings → Show the calendar bar**. It's one switch for the whole table.
Without the bar, players can't see today's weather there and don't have a way
to open the Vald calendar. You can still open the calendar from **Calendar** on
the Dashboard.

**Tucking it away.** A tucked bar shows only a thin edge. Point at it and it
slides down, and it tucks away again when you move off. Press **Y** to pull it
down and keep it there, and press it again to tuck it back. You can change the
key in Configure Controls.

The Warden can tuck it for the whole table with **Configure Settings → Air
Bladder → Configure General Settings → Tuck the calendar bar for everyone**.
Anyone can still choose for themselves with Configure Settings → Air Bladder →
**Tuck the calendar bar**: **As the Warden set it** (where everyone starts),
**Tucked** or **Not tucked**. Your own choice wins over the Warden's.

## Moving the clock

The controls are at the top of the Tables page of the
[Warden's Dashboard](warden-dashboard.md), the button at the top left of the
screen.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Back a Watch** | Takes back eight hours, for when a watch was counted twice |
| **Advance a Watch** | Moves everyone on by one watch |
| **Advance a Day** | Three watches at once |
| **To Next Morning** | Skips whatever is left of today and lands at the start of the next morning watch |
| **Set the Date…** | Type a year, month, day and watch. A line under the fields shows the date you're about to set and how many days away it is |

There's no Make Camp button. Making camp is a Wilderness Action the players
choose, and the system doesn't apply rules on a player's behalf. You move the
clock, and the party decides whether they camped.

Moving the clock doesn't post anything to chat.

## Roll Today's Weather

**Roll Today's Weather**, under the clock on the Dashboard, rolls the weather
table for the current season.

It's different from the other table buttons in two ways. The card goes to
everyone, whatever **Who sees the result** says. The result also becomes
today's weather. It shows under the calendar bar, and on the Vald calendar when
that's on, and it clears when the day turns over. If you want a private look at
a season's weather, roll that season's table on the Travel tab, which doesn't
set anything.

**Set the Weather…** beside it is for weather that isn't on any table. Type
what the sky is doing, or pick one of the season's own rows from the
suggestions. Leave the field empty to clear the weather. It doesn't post
anything to chat. With the calendar bar off, players only see the weather if
you keep a weather log.

## Keeping a weather log

Turn on **Configure Settings → Air Bladder → Configure General Settings → Keep
a weather log**. It's off by default. With it on, every time the weather is
rolled or set, a line goes into a journal called **The Weather Log**. The line
has the date, the watch, the season, anything the calendar marks on that day,
and the weather. Each month gets its own page.

Everyone at the table can read it. It's an ordinary journal, so you can edit a
line, and if you delete the journal a new one starts the next time the weather
is rolled or set.

A line is written in the language of the Warden who was running the game at the
time, and it stays in that language.

## The Vald calendar

Vald keeps its own year. It's off by default. Turn it on in **Configure
Settings → Air Bladder → Configure Hacks → Use the Vald calendar and seasons**.

With it on, the clock reads a Vald date and the Travel tab shows a **Weather in
Vald** group.

### The year

Twelve months of twenty-four days make 288 days in a year. A week is six days,
so a month is four weeks and every month begins on Market Day.

**Days of the week:** Market, Garden, Song, Tithe, Bathing, Resting.

**Months:** Mourning, Silence, Veil, Sunrise, Bright, Ashfall, Flood, Highwater,
Rise, Quell, Bane, Sunset.

**Seasons**, each about seventy-two days: **Dead** begins on the 4th of
Mourning, **Dry** on the 4th of Sunrise, **Wet** on the 4th of Flood, and
**Harvest** on the 1st of Quell.

Every tenth year adds **Reclamation**, a six-day week that doesn't belong to
any month: Recognize, Remember, Reward, Rejoice, Relinquish, Renew. A new world
starts on the 1st of Mourning, 7728, and 7728 is a Reclamation year.

### Two places the Warden's Guide disagrees with itself

Both are in the published text. This system follows the dated entries and
doesn't correct the prose.

- The Guide says each season lasts seventy-two days. Its own "season begins"
  dates make Dead and Dry seventy-two, but **Wet sixty-nine and Harvest
  seventy-five**.
- It puts *Lift the Veil*, "the end of the Dead season", on the 9th of Veil,
  **nineteen days before** Dry begins.

### Vald weather

Four tables, one per season, sit on the Travel tab under **Weather in Vald**.
They describe the day: *Light snow*, *Thunderstorms*, *Warm and breezy*.

With the Vald calendar on, Cairn's own weather buttons (the four seasons and
Weather Difficulty) leave the Travel tab. Cairn's five tables are still in the
compendium if you want a severity roll.

**Roll Today's Weather** picks the Vald table while the Vald calendar is on,
and a Cairn one while it is off.

### The month at a glance

Click the date on the calendar bar, or press **Calendar** on the Dashboard, and
the month opens. It shows twenty-four days in six columns, with today circled
and the day you last clicked highlighted. Everyone can open it while the
calendar bar is shown. With the bar off, only you can, from the Dashboard.

- **Each day is tinted with its season**, and the day a season begins carries
  that season's mark. Vald's seasons change in the middle of a month.
- **Days with something on them carry a dot.** Click one and its festival
  shows underneath, in the Warden's Guide's words. The Splash Festival and the
  Storm Dance run for three and five days, and every day of the run is marked.
- **Reclamation**, in a year that has one, is a row of six cells named Recognize
  through Renew, set apart from the months.
- **Today's weather** shows beside the season, for today only. Only you see the
  pencil beside it, which sets the weather.
- **Set the world to this day** is at the foot of the panel, and only you see
  it. Clicking a day only opens it and doesn't move the clock. The button asks
  first, and names the day and how many days away it is. The watch doesn't
  change.

The 24 festivals also ship in the **Vald** compendium as a journal entry,
*Festivals of Vald*, which you can read, edit or hand to your players.

### Putting your own days on it

**Add an event…** sits beside Set the world to this day, and only you see it.
Pick a day and say what happens.

- **Which watch** it happens in, or all day.
- **How many days** it runs.
- **Every year** makes it recur. If you leave it unticked, it belongs to the
  year you put it in.
- **The party can see it** decides which of two journals it goes in: *The
  Warden's Calendar*, which everyone can read, or *The Warden's Calendar
  (hidden)*, which players can't.

Your events show with a coloured dot and a rule down the side, apart from the
Guide's festivals. Each one is an ordinary journal page. **Edit** opens it and
**Remove** deletes it.

A hidden event stays off the party's calendar and out of their journal sidebar.
It isn't secure against a determined player, because Foundry still sends the
journal to their browser. Keep anything that must not be readable outside the
world.

### Switching it on and off

Switching the Vald calendar on doesn't convert anything. The same moment is
read as a different date, so the date on screen jumps the first time. Switching
it off brings back the date you had before.

After turning it on, use **Set the Date…** once to put the party where you want
them.

Foundry reloads when you change this setting, so everyone at the table changes
over together.
