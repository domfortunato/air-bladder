# Item & container icons

The SVG icons in this folder are from **[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net)**,
used under the **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)** licence:
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>.

They are the class icons shown for gear, spellbooks, transports, containers and
monsters throughout the system. Each is a white glyph on a black field; the
system does not recolour them.

## Attribution
